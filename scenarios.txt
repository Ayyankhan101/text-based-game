# Wolf Pack Survival Adventure Game - Scenarios

## Introduction Scenarios
1: You awaken in a misty forest, echoes of your ancestors whispering in your mind. The First Pack calls to you - a legendary wolf alliance that once ruled these lands. But hunger gnaws at you. Do you heed the echoes or hunt for survival?
   A: Follow the ancestral echoes (Spirit +10)
   B: Ignore echoes and hunt (Energy -5)

## Echoes Path
2: The echoes lead you to an ancient stone circle, glowing with ethereal light. A spirit wolf appears, offering guidance. 'Join the spirits or claim the wild?' it asks.
   A: Embrace the spirits (Spirit +20, Energy -10)
   B: Reject spirits, mark territory (Reputation +15)

4: The spirits grant you visions of the First Pack's glory. But they demand a sacrifice - your connection to the mortal world weakens.
   A: Accept visions (Spirit +30, Health -20)
   B: Pull away (Spirit +10, normal)

8: Visions show you the path to the First Pack's den, but spirits possess you briefly, draining your life force.
   A: Follow vision to den (Spirit +40, Health -40)
   B: Resist and hunt (Spirit +20, Hunger -10)

16: You reach the First Pack's den. Spirits welcome you as guardian.
   Ending: Spirit Ally: Eternal peace with ancestors.

9: You break free, but the spirits curse you with weakness. A lone traveler approaches.
   A: Attack traveler (Reputation -20, Hunger -50)
   B: Spare traveler (Spirit +15, Energy -5)

## Hunt Path
3: You stalk a deer through the underbrush, but the forest seems alive with unseen eyes. A rival wolf pack watches from the shadows.
   A: Confront the pack (Reputation +10, Health risk)
   B: Steal prey and flee (Hunger -20, Spirit -5)

6: The pack alpha challenges you. Prove your worth or submit?
   A: Challenge alpha (Health -25, Reputation +30)
   B: Submit and join (Spirit -15, Pack +1)

7: You escape with the prey, but the pack pursues. A storm brews overhead.
   A: Weather the storm (Health -15, Hunger -30)
   B: Seek shelter in ruins (Energy -10, Spirit +5)

## Territory Path
5: You howl to claim the land, but a territorial bear responds. Fight or flee?
   A: Fight the bear (Health -30, Reputation +20)
   B: Flee and hide (Energy -15, Spirit -10)

10: You defeat the bear but are gravely wounded. Echoes guide you to a healing spring.
   A: Drink from spring (Health +50, Spirit +10)
   B: Avoid spring (Health -10, Spirit -5)
   17: The spring heals you completely. You become a legendary healer. (Healer of the Wild: Restored balance.)

11: You hide in shadows, but the bear's roar shakes your resolve. Ancestors whisper of cowardice.
   A: Embrace shame (Spirit -20, Energy +10)
   B: Deny weakness (Spirit +5, Health -5)
   18: You encounter a rival pack. They challenge your authority.
      A: Fight for dominance (Reputation +20, Health risk)
      B: Submit and join (Spirit -20, Pack +1)

## Pack Challenge Path
12: You defeat the alpha! The pack submits. But leadership brings new burdens.
   A: Lead with strength (Reputation +40, Spirit -10)
   B: Lead with wisdom (Spirit +20, Energy -10)

13: You join the pack as beta. Loyalty or betrayal?
   A: Prove loyalty (Spirit +10, Pack grows)
   B: Plot betrayal (Reputation -15, Spirit -20)

## Storm Path
14: The storm rages, testing your endurance. Lightning reveals a spirit totem.
   A: Claim totem (Spirit +25, Health -20)
   B: Leave it (Spirit -5, Energy +5)
   19: A mysterious cave glows with ethereal light. Ancient runes cover the walls.
      A: Enter the cave (Spirit +15, Energy -20)
      B: Avoid the cave (Energy +5, Spirit -5)
      24: Inside the cave, you find ancient wisdom that guides you to inner peace. (Cave Wisdom: Achieved spiritual enlightenment.)
      22: The moonlight reveals a path through the forest that seems to shimmer.
         A: Follow the moonlit path (Unknown outcome)
         B: Stay on the main trail (Safe but slow)
         27: The moonlit path leads to a clearing where the First Pack once gathered. (Moon Path: Discovered ancient pack grounds.)

## Ruins Path
15: Ruins hold ancient secrets. A guardian spirit blocks your path.
   A: Bargain with spirit (Spirit +30, Energy -20)
   B: Fight spirit (Health -35, Reputation +25)
   20: You discover an abandoned wolf den. Signs suggest it was recently inhabited.
      A: Investigate the den (Energy -10, Find item)
      B: Leave it be (Spirit -5, No risk)
      25: The abandoned den contains supplies left by previous wolves. You're not alone. (Supply Discovery: Found resources for survival.)

## Additional Scenarios
21: A wounded wolf pup cries for help. Its pack is nowhere to be seen.
   A: Help the pup (Spirit +20, Energy -10)
   B: Leave it (Energy +5, Spirit -15)
   26: Helping the pup leads to reuniting it with its pack. They accept you as an ally. (Pup Rescue: Gained pack allies through compassion.)

## Endings
23: You successfully integrate into the rival pack. Your strength earns respect. (Pack Integration: Found your place in a new pack.)
